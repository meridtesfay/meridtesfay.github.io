Lead Project
MSc Thesis · 2024–2026

Brain CT Multi-Label Pathology Classification

Two-stage SSL pipeline on 500 clinical CT volumes. Stage 1: SwinUNETR Masked Autoencoder for 3D volumetric pretraining. Stage 2: SimCLR, BYOL, and Barlow Twins at slice level with MIL and gated attention pooling. 155 controlled experiments across architectures and SSL objectives. Clinical collaboration with ASUIT Trento Hospital Radiology Department.

Supervisors: Prof. Farid Melgani, Dr. Selene Tomassini. Evaluated SwinUNETR MAE pretraining against slice-level contrastive objectives with systematic ablation over batch sizes, projection heads, and augmentation strengths.

CR-MEMO: Consistency-Regularised Test-Time Adaptation

Extended MEMO (NeurIPS 2022) with a KL-divergence consistency term penalising disagreement across augmented test views. Achieved +9.12% accuracy on ImageNet-V2. Full ablation study included. Open-source modular implementation.

Independent Research · Jun 2026

Financial Trade Anomaly Detection

Two-stage pipeline for detecting fraudulent transactions at 0.17% fraud rate. Stage 1: Isolation Forest (unsupervised prior). Stage 2: XGBoost with SMOTE. Ensemble flags only when both agree, achieving 0.952 precision to minimise false positives. SHAP explainability included.
